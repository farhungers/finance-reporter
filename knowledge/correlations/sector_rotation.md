# Sector rotation

## Core rule
Sectors rotate leadership through the business cycle. Financials and cyclicals lead early expansion, tech and consumer discretionary lead mid-cycle, energy and materials lead late-cycle, and staples/utilities/healthcare defend into contraction. Relative-strength changes are the leading tell of regime shifts.

## Base rates
- Early cycle (post-recession, easing done): financials, consumer discretionary, industrials outperform ~60-70% of 12-month windows.
- Mid cycle: tech and communication services take leadership; broadest market participation.
- Late cycle: energy, materials, industrials outperform as inflation and capacity tighten; defensives start to bid.
- Contraction / recession fears: staples, utilities, healthcare outperform as investors seek earnings stability.
- Sector rotation typically leads price-level regime changes by 1-3 months in the equity index.

## Rate-sensitivity by sector
- Highly rate-sensitive (rally on falling yields): utilities, REITs, high-multiple tech (long-duration cash flows).
- Positively correlated with rising yields: financials (net interest margin), energy (often coincident with inflation).
- Mostly neutral: staples, healthcare (idiosyncratic drivers dominate).
- Rising yields hurt growth-stock multiples more than value-stock multiples — a mechanical duration effect.

## When it works
- Clear cycle positioning — cycle rules apply when macro read is coherent.
- Multi-month horizons — sector rotation is a slower signal than tactical trades.
- Confirmation from breadth — sector ETF new highs / new lows aligning with sector-leadership hypothesis.
- Combined with earnings-season catalysts — sector reactions to earnings confirm or invalidate rotation thesis.

## When it fails
- Central-bank-driven rallies where "everything goes up" (2020-2021) — rotation flattens.
- Idiosyncratic megacap-driven index moves — 5 stocks can distort sector performance.
- Rapid regime transitions — historical cycle map breaks during unusual regimes (COVID, post-COVID stimulus, 2022 inflation).
- Short-horizon tactical trades — rotation logic is weeks-to-months, not intraday.

## Application
- Blue-chip pitch bias: prefer names in currently-leading sectors for long pitches; contrarian shorts in lagging sectors need explicit `catalyst_proximity` justification.
- Weekly prep "sector/theme to watch": name the currently-leading sector and one lagging sector, with 1-line rationale.
- Rate-sensitivity check: for any rate-sensitive sector pitch, thesis must reference the current 10Y yield direction.
- `macro_alignment` rubric point: trade direction should match sector's current leadership status; mismatch = point off.
