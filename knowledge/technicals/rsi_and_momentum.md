# RSI and momentum

## Core rule
RSI(14) measures rate-of-change, not overbought/oversold in absolute terms. In strong trends RSI stays >70 for weeks — treating that as "sell" is a classic amateur error. Use RSI for DIVERGENCES and REGIME identification, not as a binary signal.

## Base rates
- Standard thresholds: >70 overbought, <30 oversold — accurate in ranging markets, misleading in trends.
- In strong uptrends, RSI oscillates 40-90; the 40-50 zone becomes support (buy-the-dip signal), not 30.
- In strong downtrends, RSI oscillates 10-60; the 50-60 zone becomes resistance.
- Bearish divergence (price higher high, RSI lower high) resolves in reversal or consolidation ~55-65% of the time within 10 sessions.
- Bullish divergence at oversold extremes (<30) has higher win rate than divergence in mid-range.
- MACD zero-line crosses lag price by 5-15 sessions on average — confirmation tool, not entry trigger.

## Base thresholds by asset class
- Equity indices: 70/30 standard; trending regimes shift to 80/40 and 60/20.
- Individual megacaps: 75/25 in normal regimes; blue-chip momentum names sustain >70 for weeks.
- Commodities: 70/30 standard; gold and oil respect divergences well.
- Crypto: 80/20 more useful thresholds — crypto extends further before mean-reverting.

## When it works
- Ranging markets — classic 70/30 mean reversion signals work.
- Divergences confirmed by price-structure break (lower high + trendline break > divergence alone).
- Multi-timeframe divergence (daily + weekly aligning) — stronger signal.
- Combined with S/R — RSI oversold AT a support level is meaningfully different from RSI oversold in mid-range.

## When it fails
- Trending markets — RSI can stay pinned >70 or <30 for weeks; selling every overbought = death by a thousand cuts.
- Around catalysts — momentum extremes get more extreme through the event.
- Choppy low-vol regimes — RSI hovers 40-60 producing no useful signal.
- Illiquid crypto small-caps — RSI whipsaws on single large prints.

## Application
- Rubric use: RSI extreme AT a real S/R level supports the `technical_setup` point; RSI extreme in isolation does NOT.
- Divergences deserve a mention in thesis prose when present, especially on 5/5-star pitches.
- Never use "RSI = 72, therefore short" as a trade rationale — that's the amateur trap.
- MACD as confirmation-only in report prose; do not build entries around MACD crosses.
