# GOOGL — Facts

## Business
- Digital advertising + cloud infrastructure + consumer platforms; Alphabet holding structure.
- Revenue mix (10-K): Google Search & Other ~56-58%, YouTube ads ~10-11%, Google Network ~9-10%, Google Cloud (GCP + Workspace) ~12-14%, Google Subscriptions/Platforms/Devices ~10-11%, Other Bets <1%.
- Ads (Search + YouTube + Network) drive ~76-78% of total revenue and >95% of operating income.
- Company-wide gross margin ~57-58%; operating margin ~32-34%; Services segment operating margin 38-40%; Cloud segment operating margin now positive but volatile.
- GOOGL (Class A, voting) vs GOOG (Class C, non-voting) — economically equivalent; spread trades within +/-1%.
- ~$70-80B/yr capex on data centers, TPUs, and AI infrastructure — structural step-up since 2023.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 5-6%; realized usually clusters near implied.
- Search revenue growth is the anchor — deceleration below 10% Y/Y triggers multiple compression regardless of Cloud strength.
- Cloud growth acceleration/deceleration is the secondary move driver; investors weight against Azure/AWS delta.
- IV crush post-earnings 45-55% front-week.
- Seasonal: Q4 is typically strongest (holiday retail ad spend); Q1 is weakest sequentially.
- Guidance behavior: no explicit revenue guide; qualitative color on ad spend + Cloud + capex.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Apr, late-Jul, late-Oct, late-Jan/early-Feb.
- Google I/O (May) — product/AI announcements; can drive multi-day narrative moves.
- DOJ antitrust cases: Search monopoly remedies phase, Ad-tech case — episodic and material; ruling days can drive 3-5% moves.
- EU Digital Markets Act enforcement — episodic negative.
- Ad-spend indicators (Meta prints, Snap/Pinterest guides) — proxy read on the digital ad tape.
- AI-search competition (ChatGPT, Perplexity) — recurring bear catalyst; typically fades within a quarter.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.1; beta to QQQ ~1.0.
- Correlation with META 0.60-0.75 (shared digital-ad exposure).
- Correlation with MSFT 0.55-0.65 (shared cloud/AI narrative).
- Sensitive to 10Y yield via long-duration DCF; correlation with rates strengthens during risk-off regimes.
- ~50% revenue ex-US; DXY headwind is meaningful but flagged consistently — priced-in.

## Common wrongness
- "AI will kill Search" — narrative recurring since ChatGPT launch; Search revenue growth has held above 10% Y/Y through the disruption window.
- "Cloud will never catch AWS/Azure" — narrative recurring since 2018; GCP has taken share, especially in AI workloads and multi-cloud.
- Overreacting to DOJ headlines — remedy phases historically move slowly; initial reaction usually overshoots.
- Underestimating YouTube monetization — Shorts and connected-TV monetization ramp has repeatedly surprised upward.
- Ignoring capex/OpEx ratio — Street rewards efficiency commentary as much as revenue growth in the current regime.
