# NVDA — Facts

## Business
- Semiconductor design (fabless); dominant merchant supplier of AI training/inference GPUs.
- Revenue mix (10-K): Data Center ~85-88%, Gaming ~7-9%, Professional Visualization ~1-2%, Automotive ~1-2%, OEM/Other ~1%.
- Data Center customer concentration: hyperscalers (MSFT, META, GOOGL, AMZN, ORCL) drive ~40-50% of DC revenue; sovereign AI + enterprise are the diversification narrative.
- Gross margin 72-76% (peaked at ~78% during Blackwell ramp); operating margin 60-63%.
- Fabless — depends on TSMC for advanced-node capacity and CoWoS packaging; supply constraint is the recurring gating factor.
- Product cadence shifted to annual (Hopper → Blackwell → Rubin → Feynman); each cycle drives ASP step-up.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 8-11%; realized frequently exceeds — the highest-vol mega-cap on earnings.
- Beat magnitude has decayed vs 2023-2024 peak; whisper numbers now anchor above sell-side consensus.
- Data Center revenue is the only line that moves the stock materially; Gaming beat/miss ignored.
- Guidance behavior: gives explicit next-quarter revenue midpoint — guide-vs-Street is the trade, not the print.
- IV crush 55-70% front-week; the largest single-stock IV crush in mega-cap complex.
- Seasonal: fiscal year ends late-January; Aug/Nov/Feb/May earnings pattern.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Feb, late-May, late-Aug, late-Nov.
- GTC conference (March) — Jensen keynote; product-roadmap and customer-testimonial venue; can drive multi-day trends.
- Computex (late-May/June, Taipei) — Asian supply-chain and product overlap; moderate move driver.
- CES (early-Jan) — automotive/robotics reveals; low direct impact.
- Hyperscaler capex prints (MSFT, META, GOOGL, AMZN earnings) — real-time read on NVDA demand; ranks above sell-side notes.
- Export controls (US → China) — episodic negative; H20/derated-part revenue is now segregated line.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.7; beta to SOX (semi index) ~1.3.
- Correlation with SOX 0.75-0.85; leadership relationship — NVDA sets the semi tape, not follows.
- Correlation with hyperscaler basket (MSFT/META/GOOGL/AMZN) 0.55-0.70 driven by shared AI-capex narrative.
- Sensitive to long-duration risk-free rate; 10Y yield spikes >20bps in a session are typically negative for NVDA relative to SPX.
- Inverse correlation with USD/CNY escalations and China semi-export headlines.

## Common wrongness
- "AI capex is peaking" — narrative recurs every 1-2 quarters since 2023; each time hyperscaler prints have reset higher.
- "Custom silicon (TPU/Trainium/MTIA) will displace NVDA" — recurring since 2016; NVDA share has held because of software stack (CUDA) lock-in.
- Overreacting to single-hyperscaler capex softness — one customer's cut is usually reallocated within the group.
- Underestimating supply-side gating — demand narratives ignore that CoWoS/HBM capacity, not orders, sets near-term revenue.
