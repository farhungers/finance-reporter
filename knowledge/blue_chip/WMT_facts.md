# WMT — Facts

## Business
- Largest US retailer by revenue; consumer staples with grocery as ~55-60% of US sales — a genuine defensive tape.
- Revenue mix: Walmart US ~68%, Walmart International ~18%, Sam's Club ~13%; e-commerce ~15% of total and growing double-digit.
- Gross margin ~24%; operating margin ~4% — thin margins, but scale advantages let modest margin expansion drive material EPS growth.
- Revenue growth engines: e-commerce (marketplace + fulfillment), advertising (Walmart Connect), membership (Walmart+), third-party services.
- Higher-income customer share has risen materially since 2022 — the "trade-down" thesis is structural, not cyclical.
- Fiscal year ends late-January.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 4-6%; realized often smaller — WMT is a low-volatility, high-quality print.
- Reports late-Feb, mid-May, mid-Aug, mid-Nov — always one of the first major retail prints each cycle, setting the tone for the consumer sector.
- Guidance behavior: gives explicit full-year comp + EPS ranges; typically raises through the year.
- Strongest calendar quarter Q4 (holiday); Q1 is historically weakest.
- Post-earnings drift: positive beats extend 3-5 trading days; negative reactions mean-revert faster than most sector peers.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Feb, mid-May, mid-Aug, mid-Nov.
- Monthly retail sales (Census Bureau, mid-month) — sector proxy.
- Consumer confidence + real wage data — WMT customer income sensitivity.
- SNAP/EBT policy changes — meaningful for the grocery mix at the low-income cohort.
- Import tariff commentary — WMT sources heavily from China/Vietnam; tariff regime shifts move gross margin guides.
- Fuel + freight costs — direct cost line and grocery-adjacent consumer income sensitivity.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.5-0.6; classic low-beta defensive.
- Correlation with XLP typically 0.60-0.75.
- Inverse correlation with consumer confidence during recessions — WMT gains share in downturns.
- Weak correlation with 10Y yield; not a duration name.
- Modest inverse to DXY on multi-week horizons via ~15% ex-US revenue.

## Common wrongness
- "Amazon will crush WMT" — recurring since 2015; WMT e-commerce growth has consistently outpaced AMZN retail in percentage terms since 2020.
- "Higher-income customer share will reverse in a strong economy" — the mix shift has persisted through improving labor markets.
- Underestimating advertising as a margin lever — Walmart Connect is a high-margin annuity being built quietly.
- "Grocery inflation rollover kills the comp" — real dollar volume growth has offset ticket normalization.
