# PG — Facts

## Business
- Largest US household + personal care company; deeply diversified branded portfolio across staples categories.
- Revenue mix by segment: Fabric & Home Care ~35% (Tide, Ariel, Downy); Baby, Feminine & Family Care ~25% (Pampers, Always, Charmin); Beauty ~19% (Olay, Pantene, SK-II); Health Care ~14% (Oral-B, Crest, Vicks); Grooming ~7% (Gillette).
- Geographic mix: North America ~50%, Europe ~22%, Asia-Pacific/Greater China ~13%, Latin America ~7%, IMEA ~8%.
- Gross margin ~50%; operating margin ~23-25%.
- Growth algorithm: 3-5% organic sales growth (mix + price + volume) + margin expansion + buyback = ~7-9% EPS.
- Fiscal year ends June — reports Jul/Oct/Jan/Apr, one quarter offset from peers.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 3-4%; realized typically smaller — quintessential low-vol staple.
- Guidance behavior: explicit full-year organic sales + core EPS ranges; adjusted regularly.
- Reports late-Jul, mid-Oct, mid-Jan, late-Apr.
- Strongest fiscal quarter historically Q2 (Oct-Dec) — holiday + winter cold/flu category strength.
- Post-earnings drift is modest — PG mean-reverts to the sector tape within a week.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Jul, mid-Oct, mid-Jan, late-Apr.
- FX moves — ~50% ex-US revenue; DXY strength is the single largest EPS variable outside pricing.
- Commodity input costs (pulp, resin, chemicals, energy) — gross margin driver.
- Private-label competition trends — pressure indicator in staples cycle.
- Consumer confidence + real income data — volume growth read.
- China consumer recovery — the SK-II beauty franchise is a swing exposure.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.5-0.6; core defensive.
- Correlation with XLP 0.75-0.85; among the highest in the sector.
- Inverse to DXY on multi-week horizons via ex-US translation.
- Weak correlation with 10Y yield despite the "bond proxy" narrative — has broken in recent rate cycles.
- Inverse to consumer discretionary during recessions — PG gains share.

## Common wrongness
- "PG is a bond proxy that trades on rates" — the correlation has weakened materially since 2022; PG is now more FX + volume-driven.
- "Volume is dead post-pricing cycle" — volume has recovered as pricing normalizes, though slowly.
- Underestimating pricing power in staples — PG has demonstrated persistent ability to raise prices without volume loss.
- "China beauty is a permanent problem" — recovery is cyclical; SK-II premium tier has recovered materially.
