# CVX — Facts

## Business
- Second-largest US integrated oil major; fully integrated upstream + downstream + chemicals (via CPChem JV with Phillips 66).
- Revenue mix: Upstream (E&P) ~40% of revenue but ~70-80% of earnings depending on oil price; Downstream (refining/marketing) ~55-60% of revenue; Chemicals ~5% (JV proportional).
- Key upstream assets: Permian Basin (top US shale producer alongside XOM), Tengiz (Kazakhstan), Gulf of Mexico deepwater, Australia LNG (Gorgon, Wheatstone).
- Hess acquisition (pending / closed 2025 timeline) adds Guyana Stabroek stake alongside XOM and CNOOC — strategic centerpiece.
- Gross margin cyclical (15-25%); operating margin swings 8-18% with crack spreads and Brent.
- Dividend aristocrat status; more aggressive capital-return-per-dollar than XOM historically.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 3-4%; CVX reactions between prints are dominated by the commodity tape.
- Reports late-Jan/early-Feb, late-Apr/early-May, late-Jul/early-Aug, late-Oct/early-Nov.
- Guidance behavior: no explicit forward EPS guidance; capex + production ranges only.
- Historically stronger dividend yield vs XOM; more aggressive buyback pace.
- Post-earnings drift: minimal — stock resumes tracking Brent/WTI within 1-2 sessions.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Jan/early-Feb, late-Apr/early-May, late-Jul/early-Aug, late-Oct/early-Nov.
- OPEC+ meetings — production quota decisions.
- Weekly EIA petroleum status report (Wednesday 10:30 ET) — crude/product inventories.
- Hess acquisition finalization + Guyana arbitration outcomes with XOM/CNOOC — episodic company-specific catalyst.
- Permian Basin operational updates — well productivity + capital efficiency commentary.
- Middle East supply risk, Russia sanctions regime.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.0; the loading masks the true commodity exposure.
- 60-day correlation with Brent typically 0.65-0.85.
- Correlation with XLE 0.85-0.95; correlation with XOM 0.75-0.85.
- Modest positive correlation with DXY-weighted commodity basket.
- Inverse to 10Y real yields is weak; CVX is more inflation-hedge than duration play.

## Common wrongness
- "CVX is just a Brent proxy" — the downstream + capital-return premium adds meaningful basis vs a pure crude ETF.
- "Peak oil demand is here" — recurring since 2019; global demand has grown each year absent recession.
- "Energy transition kills the majors" — CVX has held capex discipline and returned near-record capital through the transition period.
- Underweighting Permian productivity trajectory — CVX is a top-tier operator by well IP + capital efficiency metrics.
- "Hess deal will destroy value" — deal accretion depends on Guyana arbitration but the strategic logic is sound.
