# TSLA — Facts

## Business
- Electric vehicle manufacturer + energy storage + emerging autonomy/robotics narrative.
- Revenue mix (10-K): Automotive (vehicle sales + regulatory credits + leasing) ~78-82%, Energy Generation & Storage ~10-12%, Services & Other ~8-10%.
- Automotive gross margin ex-credits has cycled 15-20% (was 25-30% at 2022 peak) — the most-watched line item.
- Regulatory credit revenue (~$1.5-2B/yr) is high-margin and lumpy; declining structurally as competitors electrify.
- Energy segment gross margin has ramped to ~25%+ — becoming a real profit contributor, not just narrative.
- FSD/Robotaxi/Optimus are optionality lines with near-zero recognized revenue but drive the majority of the terminal-value valuation.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 8-12%; realized frequently exceeds — highest-vol US mega-cap alongside NVDA.
- Delivery print (first week after quarter-end, before earnings) is the largest single scheduled catalyst; often moves stock more than earnings itself.
- Auto gross margin is the pivot line — +/-100bps vs consensus drives multi-percent moves.
- Guidance behavior: Musk narrative on FSD/Robotaxi timelines is the durable valuation driver, not near-term unit guide.
- IV crush 50-60% post-earnings.
- Historical seasonal: Q1 deliveries typically weakest (post-holiday, subsidy pull-forward reversals).

## Key catalysts
- Delivery print: first week of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct — highest event beta.
- Earnings: late-Jan, late-Apr, late-Jul, late-Oct.
- FSD version releases + robotaxi milestone events — episodic; can drive 5-10% moves.
- CyberCab / Optimus / Master Plan events — narrative-driven, low base-rate on delivering timeline commitments.
- China EV monthly wholesale data (CPCA weekly) — real-time China demand read.
- Elon-driven headlines (political, X platform, other ventures) — episodic risk factor with genuine equity impact.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.9; beta to QQQ ~1.6.
- Highest single-name-driver correlation set — often decorrelates entirely from market on Musk news.
- Weak correlation with traditional auto basket (F, GM, TM) 0.20-0.35 — market treats TSLA as tech, not auto.
- Modest correlation with NVDA on AI/autonomy narrative days 0.35-0.50.
- Sensitive to 10Y yield on multi-week horizon; long-duration story stock.
- Retail flow sensitivity is elevated vs mega-cap peers — positioning matters more than fundamentals in short windows.

## Common wrongness
- "Legacy autos will catch up on EV" — recurring since 2018; legacy EV losses have widened, TSLA share has held ~50% of US EV market.
- "FSD is 2 years away" — Musk timelines have missed by 3-8 years consistently; base rate on delivery is low but market keeps repricing anyway.
- "Delivery print will be a beat" — consensus whispers have been more accurate than sell-side estimates; the tradeable is guide vs. whisper, not consensus.
- Overweighting single-quarter margin dip — TSLA margin cycles with mix/price/factory ramps; single-quarter reads are noisy.
- See knowledge/valuation/stories_and_numbers.md — TSLA is the canonical example of story-driven valuation; Damodaran framework: distinguish narrative from arithmetic.
