# JNJ — Facts

## Business
- Diversified healthcare — pure-play pharma + medtech since 2023 (consumer segment spun as Kenvue).
- Revenue mix: Innovative Medicine (pharma) ~65% — oncology (Darzalex, Carvykti), immunology (Stelara losing exclusivity, Tremfya), neuroscience, infectious disease; MedTech ~35% — surgery, orthopedics, cardiovascular, vision.
- Gross margin ~67%; operating margin ~28-30%.
- Stelara loss-of-exclusivity (2024-2025) is the near-term headwind; growth-driver franchises (Darzalex, Tremfya, Carvykti, Erleada, Rybrevant) are the offset thesis.
- MedTech is a slower-growth durable annuity; pharma is the higher-growth swing.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 3-4%; realized typically smaller — JNJ is a low-vol quality name.
- Reports mid-January, mid-April, mid-July, mid-October — often first major pharma print each cycle.
- Guidance behavior: explicit full-year revenue + EPS ranges; typically raises through the year.
- Dividend aristocrat status; 60+ consecutive years of increases.
- Post-earnings drift: modest — JNJ resumes tracking XLV within a session or two.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: mid-Jan, mid-Apr, mid-Jul, mid-Oct.
- Stelara biosimilar competition ramp — 2024/2025 revenue erosion pace is the key debate.
- Talc litigation updates — LTL Management bankruptcy strategy + settlement talks. Episodic catalyst.
- Pipeline readouts: multiple myeloma (Carvykti expansion), Tremfya IBD indications.
- FDA advisory committee decisions on major pipeline drugs.
- IRA Medicare price negotiation — Stelara was selected in the first negotiation round.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.6-0.7; classic low-beta defensive.
- Correlation with XLV 0.55-0.70.
- Weak correlation with 10Y yield despite pharma's "duration" reputation.
- Modest inverse to DXY via ~50% ex-US revenue.
- Weak correlation with LLY — despite both being pharma, LLY is GLP-1 driven while JNJ is a diversified basket.

## Common wrongness
- "Stelara LOE is a growth killer" — the pipeline offset (Tremfya, Darzalex, Carvykti) is roughly sized to cover the erosion; consensus has typically been too bearish on offset pace.
- "Talc litigation is an existential risk" — settled or contained in every historical major pharma tort cycle. Cash overhang is real but not existential.
- Underweighting MedTech as a durable annuity — the segment gets ignored during pharma-driven news cycles.
- "IRA Medicare negotiation will crush the pharma model" — impact per selected drug has been meaningful but manageable at portfolio level.
