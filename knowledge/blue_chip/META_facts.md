# META — Facts

## Business
- Digital advertising monopoly across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger; secondary Reality Labs (VR/AR) segment.
- Revenue mix (10-K): Family of Apps ads ~97-98%, Reality Labs ~1-2%, Other Family of Apps <1%.
- Segment operating income: Family of Apps 45-50% operating margin; Reality Labs runs ~$15-18B/yr operating loss — structural drag.
- Roughly 4B monthly active people across the family; ARPU is the durable growth lever, not user growth.
- Company-wide gross margin ~80-82%; operating margin ~40-42% consolidated (~50% ex-Reality Labs).
- ~$70-80B/yr capex on AI infrastructure — Zuckerberg's "AI investment" framing is the multi-year narrative anchor.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 8-10%; realized frequently exceeds — one of the higher-vol mega-caps.
- Q1 earnings (late-Apr) has the highest historical crash-risk: 2022 (-26%) and 2024 (-15%) both came from capex guide raises without matching revenue commentary.
- Ad-price growth and impression growth are the two lines the market decomposes — both matter.
- Reality Labs loss trajectory rarely moves the stock unless it steps up materially vs guide.
- Guidance: explicit next-quarter revenue range; full-year expense range (updated quarterly).
- IV crush post-earnings ~55-60%.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Apr, late-Jul, late-Oct, late-Jan/early-Feb.
- Connect conference (Sept/Oct) — Reality Labs product event; low move impact.
- Apple ATT/privacy changes — episodic negative from 2021; largely lapped, but new iOS releases occasionally re-trigger.
- Ad-spend proxies (GOOGL, SNAP, PINS earnings) — real-time read on ad tape.
- Regulatory: EU DMA, FTC antitrust remedies, teen safety legislation — episodic; usually recovers within one quarter.
- Election-cycle ad spend — modest tailwind in even years; not decisive.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.2; beta to QQQ ~1.1.
- Correlation with GOOGL 0.60-0.75 (shared ad-market exposure).
- Correlation with SNAP/PINS/RDDT 0.40-0.55 — Meta leads the group; sentiment travels down-cap.
- Sensitive to long-duration risk-free rate on multi-week horizon.
- Weak sensitivity to DXY despite ~55% ex-US revenue — priced-in and hedged.

## Common wrongness
- "TikTok will kill Instagram" — narrative recurring since 2020; Reels monetization ramp has offset engagement pressure.
- "Reality Labs losses will force capitulation" — recurring since 2022; Zuckerberg has repeatedly reaffirmed spend; the market has learned to discount it.
- "AI capex will crush margins" — Q1 2024 selloff repeated the pattern; revenue growth has consistently absorbed the depreciation ramp.
- Overreacting to teen/safety headlines — historically 2-3 day drag, then reversion.
- Ignoring share-buyback support — $50B+ annual repurchase provides bid on drawdowns.
