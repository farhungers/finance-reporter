# LLY — Facts

## Business
- Large-cap pharma; largest US pharma by market cap; the GLP-1 franchise (tirzepatide — Mounjaro for diabetes, Zepbound for obesity) is the dominant revenue driver.
- Revenue mix: Diabetes + Obesity (tirzepatide, Trulicity, Jardiance) ~55-60% and growing; Oncology (Verzenio, Jaypirca) ~15%; Immunology (Taltz, Olumiant) ~10%; Neuroscience (Donanemab/Kisunla) ~5%.
- Gross margin ~80%; operating margin 30-35% and rising with tirzepatide mix; heavy R&D reinvestment (~25% of revenue).
- Supply is the binding constraint on obesity revenue — capacity expansions (US + Europe) are the multi-year narrative.
- Pipeline: oral GLP-1 (orforglipron), next-gen retatrutide, Alzheimer's franchise buildout.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 5-7%; realized often larger when tirzepatide guide surprises.
- Guidance behavior: explicit full-year revenue + EPS ranges; updates typically quarterly.
- Strongest quarters historically Q2 and Q4 as GLP-1 script data accumulates through payer cycles.
- Post-catalyst drift: positive Phase 3 readouts drive multi-week outperformance vs XLV.
- IV crush post-earnings ~40-50%; smaller than smaller-cap biotech binary events.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Jan/early-Feb, late-Apr/early-May, late-Jul/early-Aug, late-Oct/early-Nov.
- Weekly IQVIA/Symphony script data — real-time GLP-1 demand read; releases every Friday for prior week.
- FDA supply-status updates for tirzepatide (removed from shortage 2024) — recurring catalyst.
- Compounding pharmacy regulatory decisions — cannibalization risk for branded GLP-1s.
- Medicare price negotiation (IRA) inclusion — Jardiance already selected; tirzepatide eligibility window approaches late-decade.
- Pipeline readouts: SURMOUNT trials, oral GLP-1 Phase 3.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.7; low-beta defensive on paper but growth-stock behavior during GLP-1 momentum.
- Correlation with NVO (Novo Nordisk ADR) 0.60-0.80 during GLP-1 news cycles.
- Weak correlation with 10Y yield in normal regimes; growth-quality multiple less rate-sensitive than pure biotech.
- Weak DXY sensitivity; ~40% ex-US revenue but pricing insulates.

## Common wrongness
- "GLP-1 TAM is saturated" — recurring since 2023; script data continues to expand each quarter.
- "Compounding pharmacies will destroy pricing" — FDA enforcement has narrowed the risk materially since 2024.
- Underestimating supply-driven revenue caps — the constraint is capacity, not demand.
- "Oral GLP-1 will cannibalize injectables" — the class expands the market rather than substituting one-for-one.
