# AVGO — Facts

## Business
- Semiconductors + Infrastructure Software; among the top three US semis by market cap alongside NVDA and TSM ADR.
- Revenue mix: Semiconductor Solutions ~55-60% (networking/AI ASICs, wireless, broadband, storage), Infrastructure Software ~40-45% (VMware post-2023 acquisition, mainframe/security tools).
- AI-related revenue (custom accelerators for hyperscalers + networking silicon) is the highest-growth line; runs at a materially higher multiple than the legacy semi mix.
- Gross margin ~75% (non-GAAP, software-heavy); operating margin ~60% non-GAAP — best-in-class for a diversified semi.
- Serial acquirer model (LSI, Broadcom, CA, Symantec, VMware) — leverage cycles up post-deal, then rapid deleveraging via FCF.
- Dividend + buyback program is large and consistent; supports the stock on drawdowns.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 5-7%; realized often exceeds implied when AI guide is the swing factor.
- Fiscal year ends October — reports Dec/Mar/Jun/Sep, one quarter offset from NVDA which affects paired-trade timing.
- Guidance behavior: gives explicit next-quarter revenue guide; multi-quarter AI outlook commentary is where the reaction lives.
- Options market pre-earnings IV crush ~40-50% overnight; less severe than pure-play AI names.
- Historically strongest post-earnings drift when AI revenue guide is raised — momentum extends 5-10 trading days.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: mid-December, mid-March, mid-June, early-September.
- Hyperscaler capex commentary (GOOGL, META, AMZN, MSFT earnings) — AVGO custom-ASIC customers; direct read-through.
- NVDA earnings — sets AI sentiment; AVGO trades in sympathy but with lower beta.
- VMware integration milestones (subscription conversion progress) — software segment margin story.
- China semi export controls — AVGO less exposed than NVDA to advanced-node restrictions but not immune.
- Fed rate path — software segment carries growth-stock duration; higher rates compress the multiple.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.3; beta to SOXX ~1.1; beta to QQQ ~1.2.
- 60-90 day correlation with NVDA typically 0.60-0.80 during AI-driven regimes.
- Inverse to 10Y yield on multi-week horizons via software-multiple sensitivity.
- Weakly correlated with DXY; ~30% ex-US revenue but hedged.

## Common wrongness
- "AVGO is just an NVDA proxy" — misses the software/VMware annuity that is roughly half the profit base.
- "Custom ASIC business is a one-hyperscaler risk" — customer concentration is real but growing across GOOGL, META, and others.
- Underestimating dividend/buyback support on selloffs — capital return has been a reliable bid.
