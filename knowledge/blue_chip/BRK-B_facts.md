# BRK-B — Facts

## Business
- Diversified holding company: insurance float + wholly-owned operating businesses + large public-equity portfolio + cash pile.
- Segment structure: Insurance (GEICO, Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance, primary specialty), Railroad (BNSF), Utilities & Energy (BHE), Manufacturing/Service/Retailing (Precision Castparts, Marmon, Duracell, See's, etc.).
- Public equity portfolio historically dominated by Apple (~40-50% at peak, trimmed materially through 2024-25), plus AXP, KO, BAC, CVX, OXY, KHC.
- Cash + short-term Treasury holdings run $150-350B; largest single-line item on balance sheet in recent years.
- Insurance float ~$170B+ — the durable low-cost funding source that defines the business model.
- No dividend; buybacks executed opportunistically under Buffett/Munger-era criteria (P/B threshold, now judgment-based).

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 2-3%; realized typically inside — one of the lowest-vol mega-caps.
- Earnings-day noise is mostly non-operating (mark-to-market on equity portfolio flows through GAAP net income); operating earnings is the number to watch.
- 13F filings (mid-Feb, mid-May, mid-Aug, mid-Nov) move underlying names more than BRK itself.
- Book-value growth vs SPX total return is the multi-year benchmark Buffett cites; single-year over/underperformance is noise.
- Guidance behavior: none. Zero explicit forward guidance.
- Annual meeting (first Sat of May, Omaha) — narrative catalyst, rarely a same-day mover.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: 10-Q releases late-Feb (annual), early-May, early-Aug, early-Nov.
- 13F filing dates: 45 days after quarter-end (mid-Feb, mid-May, mid-Aug, mid-Nov).
- Annual shareholder letter (late-Feb) — narrative/philosophy anchor; rarely direct mover.
- Cash-deployment announcements — episodic; large acquisitions (>$10B) can drive multi-percent moves.
- Insurance super-cats (major hurricanes, earthquakes) — Q3 typically has hurricane exposure disclosed.
- Succession/leadership news — Greg Abel (CEO transition), Ajit Jain (insurance), Todd Combs / Ted Weschler (portfolio).

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.85 — defensively lower than the average mega-cap.
- Correlation with SPX 0.75-0.85; behaves like a lower-vol proxy for US large-cap.
- Correlation with financials basket (XLF) 0.60-0.70 via insurance + BAC/AXP holdings.
- Inverse relationship with 10Y yield direction is weak — insurance float benefits from higher short rates, offsetting duration drag.
- Cash + Treasury reset means BRK is a net beneficiary of higher-for-longer regime, unlike most equity peers.

## Common wrongness
- "Cash pile is a drag / Buffett should deploy" — recurring since 2019; the discipline has repeatedly proven correct through subsequent drawdowns.
- "Apple trim signals bearish market view" — signal is usually tax-management or position-sizing, not macro call; noise:signal ratio high.
- Overreacting to GAAP net income swings — driven by mark-to-market on equity book, not operating deterioration.
- Underestimating BHE + BNSF earnings power — the wholly-owned operating businesses drive ~$30B+ of operating income and are opaque vs public-equity holdings.
- See knowledge/valuation/multiples_playbook.md — BRK is priced on price-to-book with regime dependency; forward P/E is misleading due to lumpy investment gains.
