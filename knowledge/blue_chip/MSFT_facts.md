# MSFT — Facts

## Business
- Enterprise software + cloud infrastructure + productivity; three reporting segments.
- Revenue mix (10-K): Intelligent Cloud (Azure + server products) ~42-44%, Productivity & Business Processes (M365, LinkedIn, Dynamics) ~32-34%, More Personal Computing (Windows OEM, Xbox, Search/News, Devices) ~24-26%.
- Azure is the single most-watched line; commercial cloud gross margin ~70%; Azure growth guidance (constant currency) is the primary post-earnings move driver.
- Company-wide gross margin ~69-70%; operating margin 44-46% — best-in-class for scaled software.
- ~$80B/yr capex run-rate on AI/data-center buildout — capital intensity has structurally reset upward since 2023.
- OpenAI relationship: preferred cloud provider, revenue share, minority equity stake — Azure AI Services is the commercial expression.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move ~4-5%; realized typically clusters at the low end when Azure growth lands in-line.
- Azure growth deceleration of >200bps vs guide is the reliable negative surprise trigger; acceleration >200bps triggers upside gap.
- Seasonal: fiscal year ends June 30 → Q4 (Jul earnings) has full-year guide setup; Q1 (Oct) is the annual FY guide reset.
- IV crush post-earnings 40-55% on front-week; smaller than NVDA/TSLA.
- Guidance behavior: segment-level color with constant-currency Azure growth range — company generally guides conservatively, beat-and-raise cadence.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Oct, late-Jan, late-Apr, late-Jul.
- Ignite conference (Nov) — product/AI announcements; low intraday move impact.
- Build conference (May) — developer platform focus; low direct move impact.
- Capex disclosure updates — every 100bps of cloud margin sensitivity is a Street focal point.
- Regulatory: FTC Activision oversight, EU cloud licensing complaints, UK CMA scrutiny — episodic, rarely moves stock >2%.
- Enterprise IT spending surveys (Morgan Stanley CIO, Gartner) — leading indicator, not tradeable day-of.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.05; beta to QQQ ~0.95.
- Highest correlation among mega-caps with enterprise-software basket (CRM, ORCL, NOW) — 0.55-0.70.
- Sensitive to 10Y yield on multi-week horizon via long-duration cash-flow discount; not intraday sensitive.
- USD-strong environment is modest headwind (~50% revenue ex-US).
- Correlation with NVDA elevated during AI infrastructure narrative windows (0.55-0.70); decouples when Azure-specific news dominates.

## Common wrongness
- "Azure will lose to AWS/GCP" — recurring since 2015; Azure has taken share nearly every quarter.
- "Copilot monetization is failing" — narrative recurs on any quarter where per-seat pricing detail isn't disclosed; typically reverses within two quarters.
- Underestimating capex ROI concerns — the market punishes capex miss even when growth is intact.
- Overweighting Windows/PC weakness — MPC segment matters less each year; drag capped by mix shift.
- See knowledge/valuation/lifecycle_valuation.md — MSFT is late-stage compounder; multiple stays elevated on growth durability, not growth rate.
