# AMZN — Facts

## Business
- E-commerce + cloud infrastructure + digital advertising + logistics; three primary segments.
- Revenue mix (10-K): North America retail ~60-62%, International retail ~20-22%, AWS ~16-18%.
- Operating income mix is inverted: AWS drives ~55-65% of consolidated operating income despite ~17% of revenue.
- Advertising is the fastest-growing high-margin line — now $50B+ run-rate, ~10% of total revenue; disclosed as separate line since 2022.
- AWS gross margin ~35-38%; operating margin fluctuates 25-38% depending on capex depreciation cadence.
- Retail operating margin is the swing factor — North America 5-6%, International 2-4% in healthy regime.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 6-8%; realized clusters near implied.
- AWS growth rate (%) is the primary move driver; +/- 100bps vs consensus moves stock 3-5% intraday.
- Retail operating income is the secondary lever — margin surprise > revenue surprise.
- Guidance: explicit next-quarter revenue range and operating income range; both are watched.
- Q4 earnings (late-Jan/early-Feb) covers the holiday quarter — highest single-quarter revenue.
- IV crush post-earnings ~50%.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Apr, late-Jul, late-Oct, late-Jan/early-Feb.
- Prime Day (mid-July + often Oct) — no direct print but retail bellwether; Street uses proxies (Numerator, Adobe data).
- AWS re:Invent (late-Nov/early-Dec) — product/AI announcements; low direct move impact but sets narrative.
- Holiday quarter GMV proxies (Adobe, Salesforce e-commerce indices) — leading indicator into Q4.
- Regulatory: FTC antitrust case, EU DMA enforcement, labor/union news — episodic.
- Freight/fuel input costs — modest quarterly headwind/tailwind for retail margin.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.15; beta to QQQ ~1.05.
- Correlation with MSFT (shared cloud narrative) 0.55-0.65.
- Correlation with retail basket (WMT, COST, TGT) is looser than expected — 0.35-0.50 — because AWS + ads dominate the equity story.
- Sensitive to 10Y yield via long-duration DCF; multi-week correlation elevated during rate spikes.
- Consumer confidence + real-wage prints matter for retail narrative but rarely move stock day-of.

## Common wrongness
- "AWS will lose to Azure/GCP" — recurring; AWS growth has stabilized in high-teens, share loss is modest and priced.
- "Retail margins can't scale" — narrative pre-2023; regionalized fulfillment network + ads monetization has repeatedly beaten skeptics.
- Overweighting one-day Prime Day print noise — real read is Q3 revenue mix.
- Underestimating advertising line — ~$50B ad business trading inside a "retail + cloud" multiple is a recurring underappreciation.
- See knowledge/sectors/tech.md — AMZN sits between consumer discretionary and tech; sector reclassification debate persists but doesn't move fundamentals.
