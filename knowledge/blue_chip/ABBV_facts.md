# ABBV — Facts

## Business
- Large-cap pharma; post-Humira loss-of-exclusivity (2023 US) navigating the transition on the strength of its immunology successors.
- Revenue mix: Immunology ~45% (Skyrizi, Rinvoq — Humira successors; Humira in decline); Oncology ~15% (Imbruvica, Venclexta, Epkinly); Aesthetics ~10% (Allergan — Botox therapeutic + cosmetic, Juvederm); Neuroscience ~15% (Vraylar, Botox migraine, Ubrelvy); Eye Care + other ~15%.
- Skyrizi + Rinvoq combined revenue has crossed Humira peak in aggregate; the "post-Humira franchise reload" is the successful case study in pharma LOE navigation.
- Gross margin ~70%; operating margin ~45-50%.
- High-dividend policy (yield typically 3-4%); dividend aristocrat trajectory since AbbVie/Abbott split (2013).
- Debt load remains elevated from Allergan acquisition; deleveraging is a multi-year narrative.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 4-5%; realized typically smaller.
- Reports late-Jan/early-Feb, late-Apr/early-May, late-Jul/early-Aug, late-Oct/early-Nov.
- Guidance behavior: explicit full-year EPS range; historically conservative; typically raises through the year.
- Post-earnings drift: positive Skyrizi/Rinvoq prints extend 5-10 trading days.
- Historically strongest performance during rate-cutting cycles as dividend yield becomes more attractive vs Treasuries.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Jan/early-Feb, late-Apr/early-May, late-Jul/early-Aug, late-Oct/early-Nov.
- Skyrizi + Rinvoq quarterly revenue trajectory — the key debate on whether combined revenue clears the Humira peak durably.
- Humira biosimilar erosion pace — well-known but pace-of-decline still moves estimates.
- IRA Medicare price negotiation — Imbruvica selected in the first round; ongoing selection cycles.
- Pipeline readouts: Epkinly expansion, next-gen immunology assets.
- FDA advisory committee decisions on new indications.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.7; low-beta defensive.
- Correlation with XLV 0.55-0.70.
- Correlation with LLY is modest — different franchises.
- Weak DXY sensitivity; ~25% ex-US revenue.
- Positive correlation with defensive/high-dividend baskets during risk-off regimes.

## Common wrongness
- "Post-Humira ABBV is a value trap" — the immunology reload thesis has broadly played out; consensus was too bearish through 2022.
- "Biosimilar erosion will be faster than modeled" — the US Humira erosion has actually been slower than European pace due to PBM formulary dynamics.
- Underweighting Aesthetics as an economically-sensitive but structurally-growing franchise.
- "IRA is a growth killer" — impact per drug is material but portfolio-level offset is durable.
