# COST — Facts

## Business
- Warehouse membership retailer; ~880+ warehouses globally; ~130M+ cardholders.
- Revenue mix: Merchandise sales ~98% (food + sundries ~40%, non-food ~28%, fresh ~15%, ancillary ~15%); Membership fees ~2% of revenue but ~70% of pre-tax income.
- Membership renewal rate ~92-93% (US/Canada); executive-tier mix rising.
- Gross margin ~13% (deliberately low — passes savings to members); operating margin ~3-4%.
- Growth algorithm: high-single-digit comps + new warehouse openings + membership fee raises every ~5-7 years.
- Fiscal year ends late-August.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 4-6%; can extend when comps or membership commentary surprises.
- Reports monthly comp sales — no other US mega-cap retailer reports monthly. Creates 12 mini-catalysts per year.
- Reports quarterly earnings mid-Dec, early-March, late-May, late-Sept.
- Guidance behavior: no explicit forward guidance. Reactions hinge on membership fee timing hints + comp trajectory.
- Post-earnings drift: modest — COST is a quality compounder that resumes trend after digestion.

## Key catalysts
- Monthly comparable sales release (typically first Wednesday) — regular tape mover.
- Quarterly earnings: mid-Dec, early-March, late-May, late-Sept.
- Membership fee increase announcements — historically every 5-7 years; the current cycle raised fees in September 2024.
- Special dividend announcements (irregular, ~every 3-4 years).
- Fuel margin — Costco gas stations are a comp-store traffic driver + margin tailwind when fuel spreads widen.
- Import tariff commentary — Costco sources non-food heavily from Asia.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~0.8-0.9.
- Correlation with XLP 0.55-0.70; behaves more like quality-growth than pure staple.
- Correlation with WMT 0.50-0.65.
- Weak DXY sensitivity; ~10-15% ex-US revenue.
- Weak 10Y sensitivity; multiple trades on comp + membership durability, not rates.

## Common wrongness
- "COST valuation is unsustainably high" — recurring since 2019; the P/E has stayed elevated because comp growth + membership durability justify the premium.
- "E-commerce will erode warehouse membership" — recurring since 2015; renewal rates have expanded, not contracted.
- Underestimating fuel + ancillary services (pharmacy, optical, travel) as membership stickiness levers.
- "Inflation rollover kills the comp" — real-dollar traffic growth has offset ticket normalization.
