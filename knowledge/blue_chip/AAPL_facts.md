# AAPL — Facts

## Business
- Consumer hardware + services ecosystem; largest US company by market cap; S&P 100 top weight.
- Revenue mix (10-K): iPhone ~50-52%, Services ~22-25%, Wearables/Home/Accessories ~10-11%, Mac ~8-10%, iPad ~6-8%.
- Services (App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, AppleCare, licensing) carries ~70% gross margin vs ~35% product margin — mix shift toward Services is the long-run margin story.
- Company-wide gross margin runs 44-46%; operating margin 29-31%; industry-leading FCF conversion.
- Greater China is ~17-19% of revenue and the single largest geographic swing factor.
- Massive buyback program (~$90B/yr run-rate) is a structural share-count tailwind and EPS floor.

## Base rates
- Post-earnings implied move 4-5%; realized often lands inside. Not a reliable directional trade day-of.
- Seasonal pattern: strongest calendar Q4 (Sept iPhone launch → holiday). January-March window is historically weakest as launch-quarter comps normalize.
- IV crush post-earnings is meaningful but smaller than NVDA/TSLA — front-week ATM options lose ~50-60% of premium overnight.
- Guidance behavior: qualitative color, not hard numbers, since 2020 — Street reactions hinge on gross margin commentary and China callouts rather than headline beats.
- WWDC (June) rarely moves the stock >2% intraday absent a major platform reveal.

## Key catalysts
- Earnings: late-Jan (holiday quarter), late-Apr, late-Jul, late-Oct/early-Nov.
- September iPhone event — dates typically first or second Tuesday post-Labor Day.
- WWDC — early-to-mid June; watch for AI, OS, and Vision Pro category updates.
- China smartphone data (Counterpoint, IDC monthly) — Greater China revenue proxy.
- App Store regulatory: DOJ antitrust suit, EU Digital Markets Act enforcement, Epic case appeals — episodic negative catalyst.
- USD strength: ~60% of revenue is ex-US; strong DXY is a translation headwind flagged in nearly every earnings call.

## Correlations
- Beta to SPX ~1.2; beta to QQQ ~1.05.
- Highly correlated with mega-cap tech basket (MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN) — 90-day rolling correlation typically 0.65-0.80.
- Inverse to DXY on multi-week horizons due to translation exposure; correlation weak intraday.
- Weak sensitivity to 10Y yield vs pure-growth peers because of Services annuity + buyback support.

## Common wrongness
- "iPhone unit growth is dead" — narrative recurring since ~2016; ASP growth + Services mix has repeatedly overwhelmed unit stagnation.
- "China ban/boycott will crater revenue" — recurring post-Huawei-launch scare; usually mean-reverts within one quarter.
- Overreaction to supply-chain leaks (Nikkei, DigiTimes) — 60%+ do not materialize as reported.
- Underestimating buyback support on selloffs — $20B+ quarterly repurchase creates a bid on drawdowns.
- See knowledge/valuation/multiples_playbook.md — Apple trades on forward P/E in mid-20s and rerates with Services mix, not iPhone units.
