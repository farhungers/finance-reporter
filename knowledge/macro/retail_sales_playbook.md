# Retail sales playbook

## What it is
US Advance Monthly Retail Trade Report from the Census Bureau, released **08:30 ET around the 15th of each month**. Measures dollar sales at ~5,500 retailers.

Series markets watch, in order of importance:
1. **Control group** (retail sales ex autos, gas, building materials, food services) — feeds directly into GDP personal consumption. Most market-moving line.
2. **Headline retail sales** — full number including autos and gas.
3. **Retail sales ex autos** — cleaner monthly signal (autos are lumpy).
4. **Retail sales ex autos and gas** — cleaner still (gas is oil-price-driven).

Adjacent numbers same morning:
- Prior 2 months' revisions (increasingly negative 2023-2025).
- Advance food services and drinking places — restaurant proxy for discretionary spending.

## Why control group matters most
The control group directly maps to GDP personal consumption in the same quarter. A hot control-group print resets Wall Street's tracking GDP estimate (Atlanta Fed GDPNow) within hours. Cold prints do the reverse.

## Historical reaction
- **Hot control (≥0.4% MoM, ≥15bps above consensus):** 2Y +5-10bps, SPX +0.3% to +0.8%, USD +20-40bps. Rate-cut push-out.
- **Cold control (≤-0.2% MoM):** rate-cut odds firm; SPX reaction depends on tone — if labor is strong, mildly positive; if labor is soft, growth-scare risk dominates.
- **In-line print with big prior revisions:** revisions dominate the day. Negative prior revisions ≥0.3pp = market treats current print as weaker than headline implies.

## Playbook
- **Read control first, then check revisions, then check food services line.** Food services strong = discretionary spending intact = consumer OK. Food services weak while goods retail hot = consumer trading down.
- **Cross-check with unit vs dollar.** Retail sales are nominal dollars — high inflation months look hot even when unit demand is falling. Adjust mentally by same-month CPI (or wait for real spending in PCE ~10 days later).
- **Sector map:**
  - Hot control → consumer discretionary + banks outperform; consumer staples underperform.
  - Cold control → staples + healthcare + utilities outperform; discretionary + regional banks lag.
  - Cold + weak food services + labor cooling → recession-trade activation.
- **Position vs Amazon Prime Day / holiday windows.** July prints often distorted upward by Prime Day; November-December seasonally noisy. Look at YoY not MoM in those windows.

## Common wrongness
- **Reading headline while ignoring control.** Autos + gas swing wildly; control is the demand signal.
- **Ignoring negative revisions in a beat month.** Revisions ≥-0.3pp effectively "give back" the beat.
- **Trading nominal dollars in high-inflation months without deflating.** Nominal +0.5% at 4% inflation = real -0.5%. This distinction mattered in 2021-2022.
- **Assuming a strong month = trend.** Two consecutive months matter; single months rank low on signal.

## Reference dates for base-rate anchoring
- Retail Sales Jan 2023 (control +1.7% MoM, huge beat): SPX +1.3%, rate-cut expectations pushed back 6 weeks.
- Retail Sales May 2024 (control -0.1% miss with negative revisions): USD sold hard, SPX +0.5%.
- Retail Sales Jul 2024 (control +0.4% strong): confirmed consumer intact despite labor softening, delayed recession-trade activation by 2 months.
- Retail Sales Mar 2020 (headline -8.7% during first Covid month): confirmed depth of consumption collapse; validated Fed emergency actions.
