# ISM playbook (Manufacturing + Services)

## What they are
- **ISM Manufacturing PMI** — released **10:00 ET the first business day of each month** by the Institute for Supply Management. Survey of ~300 US manufacturing purchasing managers. Above 50 = expansion, below 50 = contraction.
- **ISM Services PMI** — released **10:00 ET the third business day of the month**. Same methodology, services sector. Services is ~80% of US GDP, so this is the larger economy read.

Both include diffusion indices for:
- **Prices paid** — leading inflation indicator (feeds PPI and CPI).
- **New orders** — leading demand indicator (feeds subsequent PMI headlines).
- **Employment** — leading NFP indicator (feeds subsequent jobs report).
- **Backlog / supplier deliveries** — supply-chain tightness gauge.

## Why ISM matters
- **First hard economic data of the month.** Sets tone for the whole month's macro releases.
- **Leading indicator.** New orders subcomponent leads headline by 1-2 months.
- **Regime signal.** ISM Manufacturing below 45 for 3+ months has preceded every US recession since 1970 (some false positives). ISM Services below 48 is more decisive.

## Alternative signal: S&P Global US PMI
S&P Global (formerly IHS Markit) releases US manufacturing and services PMIs on the **23rd** of each month (before ISM the following month). Uses ~600-company sample, slightly different methodology. Generally has smaller impact but useful cross-check:
- ISM and S&P Global diverging >2 points on the same sector → wait for the ISM print, and read the divergence as elevated uncertainty about the true trend.
- S&P Global tends to lead ISM at inflection points by 1 release (rare but historically significant).

## Historical reaction
- **ISM Manufacturing hot (≥53, ≥2 pts above consensus):** 2Y +5-10bps, SPX +0.3% to +0.7%, industrials + materials outperform.
- **ISM Manufacturing cold (≤47, ≥2 pts below consensus):** 2Y -5-10bps, SPX flat-to-down (growth scare potential), defensives outperform.
- **ISM Services hot (≥55):** larger reaction than mfg — 2Y +8-15bps, SPX +0.5%, banks + discretionary outperform.
- **ISM Services cold (≤48):** rare and market-moving — 2Y -10-15bps, SPX -0.5% to -1.5% (services is the larger economy).
- **Prices-paid surprise:** if prices-paid jumps ≥5 points regardless of headline, treated as pre-CPI inflation signal, rates react.

## Playbook
- **Read headline, new orders, employment, prices in that sequence.** Headline = current, new orders = next month, employment = next NFP, prices = next CPI.
- **Regional Fed surveys as pre-signal.** Empire (mid-month), Philly Fed (3rd Thursday), Richmond (~4th Tuesday) each cover partial ISM territory. If all 3 trend the same direction, ISM usually follows.
- **Chicago PMI** — released last business day of the month, right before ISM. Historically strong lead on ISM Mfg by 1-2 points direction.
- **Services 3rd business day matters most.** Larger economy, more employment signal, feeds NFP forecasts for the Friday of the same week when the calendar aligns.

## Common wrongness
- **Trading a single sub-50 print as recession.** 3-month rolling average matters more; single prints get revised.
- **Ignoring ISM Services on days it releases.** Larger economic weight — bigger impact per point than manufacturing.
- **Treating prices-paid as CPI-equivalent.** It's a diffusion index (share of firms reporting rising prices), not a magnitude measure. Reads pressure direction, not magnitude.
- **Ignoring the S&P Global preview 10 days earlier.** When it diverges materially, ISM reaction is amplified.

## Reference dates for base-rate anchoring
- ISM Manufacturing Sep 2022 (50.9, first sub-51 print of tightening cycle): 2Y -10bps, marked start of Q4 2022 SPX rally.
- ISM Services Nov 2023 (52.7, in-line): muted reaction (modal outcome).
- ISM Services Jan 2024 (53.4 headline hot + prices-paid surge): sparked "sticky services inflation" narrative for Q1.
- ISM Manufacturing Jul 2024 (46.8, deep miss): triggered growth-scare, SPX -1.4%.
- Chicago PMI + Empire + Philly all soft Sep 2024: ISM Mfg subsequent print confirmed, reactions modest because pre-signaled.
