# Powell speeches playbook

## The four speech types
Chair Powell's public appearances break into four categories with meaningfully different market impact.

### 1. FOMC decision-day press conference (highest impact)
- **When:** 14:30 ET after each FOMC statement (8 times/year).
- **Format:** ~10 min prepared remarks + ~50 min Q&A from journalists.
- **Impact:** Regularly moves SPX 1-2% and 2Y yield 10-20bps during the hour. See `fomc_playbook.md` for detail.
- **Key rule:** Q&A > prepared remarks. The nuance emerges in journalist questions.

### 2. Semiannual Congressional testimony (Humphrey-Hawkins)
- **When:** Twice yearly, typically **February and July**. Testifies Tuesday to Senate Banking Committee, Wednesday to House Financial Services.
- **Format:** Prepared statement (usually pre-released Monday evening) + 3-4 hours of Q&A per day.
- **Impact:** Prepared statement typically moves markets ≤0.3% (pre-released, digested overnight). Q&A can move markets 0.5-1% during the 3-hour session. Watch for specific senator/rep questions on inflation trajectory + rate-cut timing.
- **Key rule:** Certain lawmakers (Warren, Toomey historically, Cassidy currently) reliably extract news from Powell in Q&A.

### 3. Scheduled economic conference speech
- **When:** Throughout the year, at NABE, Peterson Institute, Brookings, Council on Foreign Relations, Chicago Booth, Stanford Institute, etc.
- **Format:** ~20 min prepared remarks + moderated Q&A (~15 min).
- **Impact:** Prepared remarks can move markets 0.5-1.5% if policy-substantive. Moderated Q&A can produce headlines when moderators press on specific policy questions.
- **Key rule:** Speeches with a specific policy topic (e.g., "Inflation and the labor market") have higher impact than general economic remarks.

### 4. Community bank / ceremonial appearance (lowest impact)
- **When:** Occasional throughout the year.
- **Format:** Brief remarks focused on regional economy or bank supervision. Rarely Q&A on policy.
- **Impact:** Typically <0.1%. Not market-moving. Do NOT position for these.
- **Key rule:** If the venue is a regional bank association or a ribbon-cutting, no policy news is coming.

## Blackout window
For **10 days before an FOMC meeting through the day after**, Powell is silent (as are all FOMC members). Anything scheduled to fall in that window is either cancelled or extended to include a specific waiver — worth watching if it happens.

## Historical impact — biggest Powell speech moves
- **Jackson Hole Aug 2022 (hawkish, "some pain"):** SPX -3.4% intraday. See `jackson_hole.md`.
- **Nov 2018 Economic Club NY (dovish pivot from Oct hawkish):** SPX +2.3%, 2Y -8bps. Started the "Powell put" era.
- **Nov 2023 IMF speech (unexpectedly hawkish mid-pause):** SPX -0.8%, 2Y +8bps.
- **Aug 2024 Jackson Hole (dovish signal for September cut):** SPX +1.1%. See `jackson_hole.md`.
- **Congressional testimony Mar 2023 (hawkish, "faster and higher"):** 2Y +30bps, SPX -1.5%. This was the "50bp back on the table" pivot.
- **Congressional testimony Jul 2024 (dovish on labor cooling):** 2Y -8bps, SPX +0.4%.

## Playbook
- **Read the venue first.** Community bank speech = ignore. Economic conference = attention. Testimony or FOMC presser = full engagement.
- **Watch the topic vs recent Fed narrative.** If Powell's topic is on-narrative (already the current focus), his talk usually reinforces + minimal market move. If topic diverges (e.g., a labor-focused speech during an inflation-narrative period), pay attention — new emphasis often previews policy shift.
- **Check for pre-release.** Prepared remarks sometimes go up to the Fed website an hour before delivery. If pre-released, initial reaction is muted; Q&A carries the news.
- **Cross-check with Nick Timiraos.** Timiraos's weekend WSJ articles ahead of major Powell speeches often preview the intended message. Historically 85%+ hit rate for the framing.

## Position sizing rules
- **FOMC decision-day presser:** treat as a 1-2% SPX vol event. Sizing must survive both statement and presser reactions.
- **Congressional testimony:** treat as a 0.5-1% event; typical setups can hold through it if invalidation is >0.8% away.
- **Economic conference speech:** treat as a 0.3-0.8% event; most setups can hold with normal invalidation levels.
- **Community/ceremonial:** ignore for position sizing.

## Common wrongness
- **Trading every Powell headline as if it were policy-substantive.** Only presser + testimony + major-conference speeches consistently move markets.
- **Missing the pre-release.** Reactions look smaller than they are when the news landed Sunday night, not during the speech Monday morning.
- **Reading tone from first-30-second wire summaries.** Bloomberg + Reuters real-time characterizations of "hawkish" or "dovish" are sometimes reversed within 15 minutes as more of the transcript is read.
- **Ignoring Q&A in favor of prepared remarks.** Prepared remarks are often the setup; Q&A is often where the news lives.
