# Fed speak taxonomy

## Voting vs non-voting
Vote status matters more than headline title. Non-voters rarely move markets.

## Speaker ranking (typical market impact)
1. Chair (Powell)
2. Vice Chair
3. NY Fed president (permanent voter)
4. Voting regional presidents (rotate)
5. Non-voting regional presidents (rarely move markets except when consensus-breaking)
