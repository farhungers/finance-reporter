# Jackson Hole playbook

## What it is
The Kansas City Fed's annual Economic Policy Symposium at Jackson Lake Lodge in Wyoming, held **late August (typically 3rd or 4th weekend)**. Central bankers, economists, and finance ministers from around the world attend. The event runs Thursday evening through Saturday noon.

**The Powell keynote is the market event.** Delivered **Friday morning ~10:00 ET**. Historically ~20 minutes prepared remarks + no Q&A. The symposium theme (set months in advance) frames Powell's talk.

Other Fed speakers give remarks throughout the weekend but Powell's Friday keynote is the market-moving one.

## Why it matters
- **Powell has used Jackson Hole to re-anchor policy narrative into the fall.** Free from FOMC statement conventions, he can talk more directly.
- **Timing sits in the pre-September-FOMC blackout gray zone** — the symposium falls outside the strict blackout window, so it's the last major Fed communication before September's meeting.
- **International reset.** ECB, BOE, BOJ officials also speak — cross-central-bank policy divergence often crystallizes here.

## Historical impact — Powell keynotes
- **Aug 2022 ("Some pain to households"):** SPX -3.4% on the day. Kicked off the Q3 2022 sell-off. Powell explicitly told markets to expect pain.
- **Aug 2023 ("Prepared to raise further"):** SPX -1.0%, 2Y +10bps. Confirmed hawkish stance despite peak-rates narrative starting to build.
- **Aug 2024 ("Time has come to adjust policy"):** SPX +1.1%, 2Y -8bps. Explicitly signaled September cut. This was the pivot line.
- **Aug 2010 (Bernanke QE2 preview):** SPX rallied 15% over next 3 months.
- **Aug 2014 (Yellen wage focus):** technical remarks; no market impact.
- **Aug 2020 (Powell FAIT framework):** SPX +0.7%; formalized average-inflation-targeting shift that shaped 2021-2022 policy errors.

## Playbook
- **Position for asymmetric response.** Powell rarely delivers no-news at Jackson Hole. Options implied vol on Friday morning typically prices in a 1-1.5% SPX move. Long straddles into the print have edge historically.
- **Read the symposium theme first.** If the theme is "structural" (e.g., "Reassessing constraints on the economy and policy" = 2023), expect long-term framework talk, less near-term rate signal. If theme is "cyclical" (e.g., "Reassessing the effectiveness of monetary policy" = specific), expect near-term guidance.
- **Post-keynote drift lasts 3-5 trading days.** Unlike FOMC decisions where the reaction is intra-day, Jackson Hole moves often extend through the following week as the message sinks in.
- **Cross-central-bank comparisons.** Watch for ECB or BOE speakers in the same weekend delivering aligned or divergent messages. Aligned = policy consensus (typically dollar-positive). Divergent = potential currency-vol trades.

## Sector map by Powell tone
- **Hawkish Powell (2022):** short-duration Treasuries + banks + energy outperform; long-duration tech + REITs + gold lag.
- **Dovish Powell (2024):** long-duration tech + REITs + regional banks + gold outperform; USD-cyclical staples lag.
- **Neutral / technical Powell (2020, 2014):** minimal sector rotation; realized vol crushes IV.

## Common wrongness
- **Trading the first-30-minutes reaction.** Some Powell keynotes have taken longer to re-price (extended parsing of specific wording, or overnight international reaction the following Monday).
- **Ignoring the symposium theme.** Structural themes = less near-term signal. Cyclical themes = more.
- **Under-weighting international speakers.** ECB President's Friday remarks have moved EUR-USD 100+ pips on multiple occasions.
- **Assuming absence of dovish signal = hawkish.** Silence at Jackson Hole is not a signal — Powell reserves major shifts for specific windows.
