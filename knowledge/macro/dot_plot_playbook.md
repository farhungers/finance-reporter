# Dot plot playbook (FOMC SEP)

## What it is
The Summary of Economic Projections (SEP) is released alongside the FOMC statement four times per year (**March, June, September, December** meetings). Contains each of the 19 FOMC members' individual projections for:
- Fed funds rate at end of each of the next 3 calendar years + longer-run ("terminal" rate).
- Real GDP growth (Q4/Q4).
- Unemployment rate (Q4 average).
- PCE inflation (Q4/Q4).
- Core PCE inflation (Q4/Q4).

**The "dot plot"** = the visual of all 19 members' fed funds rate projections. Each dot is one member (voter or non-voter). The **median** dot for each year is what markets fixate on.

Released at 14:00 ET with the statement. Read by traders in the first 30 seconds after 14:00.

## The three numbers markets trade
1. **Median 2025 dot** (or nearest year) — the "next 12 months" policy path.
2. **Median longer-run dot** — the neutral rate estimate. This anchors long-duration asset valuations.
3. **Dispersion (max-min range)** — signals internal disagreement. Widening = less confident consensus = more IV.

## Historical reaction (post-2022 SEP releases)
- **Median near-term dot up ≥25bps vs prior SEP:** hawkish surprise. 2Y +10-20bps, SPX -0.8% to -1.5%.
- **Median near-term dot down ≥25bps:** dovish surprise. 2Y -10-15bps, SPX +0.5% to +1.2%.
- **Median unchanged, dispersion widens:** modest hawkish tilt (uncertainty = risk-off marginally). 2Y +3-5bps.
- **Longer-run dot up 25bps** (rare but structural): hits long-duration valuations. Tech, REITs, gold underperform for 2-4 weeks.
- **Longer-run dot down:** structural bull for duration + growth.

## Playbook
- **Read median 2025 first, then longer-run, then dispersion.** This ordering matches Powell's press-conference sequencing.
- **Compare to fed funds futures pricing pre-meeting.** If futures priced ~4.0% terminal and dots show 4.25% median = hawkish surprise even if dots unchanged from last SEP. Market vs Fed is where the trade lives.
- **Watch which non-voters moved.** A non-voter's dot shift can preview their vote if they cycle onto the voting roster next year.
- **Longer-run = neutral rate = valuation anchor.** A 25bp shift up in the neutral rate justifies ~5% de-rating of long-duration equities via terminal value math.
- **Dot dispersion matters at the extremes.** When dots are in tight clusters (e.g., all 19 within 50bps for 2025), Fed is united; markets can trust the median. Wide dispersion (100bps+ range) means the Fed is genuinely uncertain and the median could shift meaningfully at the next SEP.

## Common wrongness
- **Reading dots as forecasts, not reaction functions.** Members' dots reflect what they'd do IF their macro forecast plays out. The macro forecast is what to interrogate.
- **Ignoring the longer-run dot** for near-term profits. The neutral rate matters for structural positioning; skipping it means missing the long-duration signal.
- **Trading the 14:00 dot reaction without waiting for the 14:30 presser.** Powell's tone in Q&A often re-frames what the dots meant. See `fomc_playbook.md`.
- **Assuming dots always predict outcomes.** Historical hit rate of end-of-year dots for the coming year's actual policy is only ~55% for the near-term dot and much lower for the 2-3-years-out dots.

## Sector map by dot shift
- **Near-term dot up (hawkish):** short-duration Treasuries + banks + energy outperform. Long-duration tech, gold, REITs lag.
- **Near-term dot down (dovish):** long-duration tech + REITs + gold + regional banks outperform. Value staples lag.
- **Longer-run dot up:** structural pain for long-duration + growth stocks; multi-week rotation into cyclicals + value.
- **Longer-run dot down:** structural bull for tech, biotech, unprofitable-growth cohort, gold.

## Reference dates for base-rate anchoring
- SEP Jun 2022 (median 2022 dot: 3.4% from 1.9%, huge hawkish shift): SPX -3.3%, 2Y +25bps.
- SEP Dec 2023 (median 2024 dot: 4.6%, showing 3 cuts, more dovish than expected): SPX +1.4%, 2Y -30bps.
- SEP Sep 2024 (median 2024 dot -50bps to 4.4%, dot plot confirmed cutting cycle): SPX flat (priced in), gold ripped.
- SEP Sep 2025 (dot plot showed continued easing path 2025-2026): duration outperformed cyclicals for the following month.
- SEP Mar 2024 (median 2024 dot stayed at 3 cuts vs feared 2): SPX +1.0%.
