# JOLTS playbook

## What it is
Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey — monthly BLS release covering the last business day of the prior month. Released **10:00 ET, usually first Tuesday of the month** (before NFP that Friday). Four numbers matter:

1. **Job openings (total)** — number of unfilled US positions. Peak was ~12M in Mar 2022; trend around 7-8M in 2025.
2. **Hires** — jobs filled during the month.
3. **Quits** — voluntary separations. High quits = worker confidence = wage pressure.
4. **Layoffs & discharges** — involuntary separations. Rising = late-cycle weakness signal.

Derived metric markets watch:
- **Vacancy-to-unemployed ratio (V/U)** — openings ÷ unemployed persons. Peaked at 2.0 in 2022, ~1.1 in 2025 (near pre-Covid 1.2). Powell cited this repeatedly 2022-2023 as the labor-tightness measure.
- **Quits rate** — quits ÷ total employment. Above 2.5% = labor tight, wage pressure. Below 2.0% = labor slack.

## Why JOLTS matters
- **Fed's stated labor-tightness gauge** — Powell has cited V/U ratio and quits rate in nearly every 2022-2024 press conference.
- **Leads NFP.** Hiring intensity in JOLTS pre-signals payroll changes 1-3 months out.
- **Two-month lag though.** JOLTS covers month M-2 (released in month M) — freshness gap vs weekly claims and monthly NFP.

## Historical reaction
- **Big JOLTS drop (openings ≥300k below prior):** 2Y yield -5-12bps, rate-cut odds +5-10%. SPX modestly bid on rate-cut relief, unless...
- **Big drop + rising layoffs subcomponent:** growth-scare tone kicks in — SPX flat-to-down while rates rally hard.
- **Big JOLTS beat (openings ≥300k above prior):** 2Y +5-8bps, rate-cut push-out. USD +20-30bps.
- **Modal outcome (openings within ±150k of prior):** minimal market reaction; report used for internal Fed modeling.

## Playbook
- **Read V/U ratio first, then quits rate.** Direction of V/U tells you the labor-tightness trend; quits rate tells you the wage-pressure feed.
- **Layoffs subcomponent = late-cycle antenna.** A JOLTS report with declining openings AND rising layoffs is the most bearish combination for cyclicals + banks.
- **Position against NFP week.** JOLTS on Tuesday sets narrative for the Friday NFP print. Cold JOLTS + cold NFP = strong dovish momentum; cold JOLTS + hot NFP = confused signal that unwinds by following week.
- **Sector map:**
  - Cold JOLTS + growth scare tone → long-duration Treasuries + defensives + gold outperform.
  - Cold JOLTS + rate-relief tone (no rising layoffs) → tech + housing + regional banks outperform.
  - Hot JOLTS → banks + industrials outperform; long-duration tech + REITs lag.

## Common wrongness
- **Trading JOLTS as if it were the current month.** It's 2 months old data. Weekly initial claims are more current for a labor pulse.
- **Ignoring layoffs subcomponent when openings drop.** The combination matters, not either in isolation.
- **Reading total quits without normalizing to the rate.** Total employment grows over time; use quits rate.

## Reference dates for base-rate anchoring
- JOLTS Sep 2022 (openings -1.1M vs prior month, largest single-month drop ever): 2Y -20bps, SPX +3% within 24h.
- JOLTS Jul 2024 (openings dropped to 7.7M from 8.2M): confirmed labor cooling, rate-cut Sep pricing firmed.
- JOLTS Mar 2022 (openings 11.9M, peak): Powell cited in June 2022 presser as evidence of labor tightness requiring aggressive policy.
- JOLTS Nov 2023 (openings 8.79M, first breach of 9M level for 6 months): confirmed Fed's "gradual cooling" narrative into Dec FOMC.
