# PPI playbook

## What it is
Producer Price Index — measures the average change over time in the selling prices received by US domestic producers for their output. Released by BLS at **08:30 ET, typically the day before or day after CPI** (release schedule interleaves them within a 2-day window).

Two headline series:
- **Headline PPI final demand MoM/YoY** — all producer prices.
- **Core PPI (ex food, energy, and trade services) MoM/YoY** — cleaner trend series.

Also watch **PPI healthcare services** — feeds directly into the PCE healthcare component via BEA's imputation.

## Why PPI matters
- **Leading indicator for CPI/PCE by 1-2 months.** Producer input prices → consumer output prices with lag depending on pricing power. Big PPI moves that don't reverse within a month often become CPI moves.
- **Pipeline pressure gauge.** Rising PPI with flat CPI = margin compression at retailers (bad for consumer discretionary). Rising PPI with rising CPI = pricing power intact (bullish for those with brands).
- **PCE healthcare feed.** PPI physician + hospital services flow into PCE healthcare via BEA imputation. Big PPI-healthcare surprise pre-signals PCE the following week.

## Historical reaction
- **Muted at first, amplified in follow-on prints.** PPI typically moves markets 30-50% as much as an equivalent CPI surprise on release day. But the 2-month CPI feed-through often produces a larger secondary move.
- **Hot PPI + upcoming CPI in 1-2 weeks:** buy-side positioning shifts hawkish going into CPI, amplifying subsequent CPI reaction.
- **Cool PPI with hot CPI already released:** treated as "next month's CPI already looking better" — mild bond rally, modest SPX bid.

## Playbook
- **Sequence matters.** If PPI releases FIRST in the CPI/PPI pair (~40% of months), CPI gets read against the PPI signal. If CPI releases first, PPI is the confirmation.
- **Watch trade services subcomponent.** Includes retailer margins — big swings signal shifting pricing power. This is the "why did the ex-trade-services line get excluded" answer for the current month.
- **Cross with import prices** (released day of or day after PPI in most months). Import prices weak + PPI weak = tariff/dollar deflationary force operating. Import prices hot + PPI hot = broad supply-side pressure.
- **PPI-healthcare pre-signals PCE.** If PPI healthcare surprises up meaningfully, prep for PCE surprise up ~10 days later.

## Common wrongness
- **Treating PPI reaction like CPI reaction.** PPI moves less intra-day but sets up bigger positioning shifts for the coming CPI/PCE.
- **Ignoring trade services.** The line is volatile but signals shifting retailer pricing power, which has second-order sector implications.
- **Reading YoY without base effects.** PPI YoY comparisons often distorted by 2021-2022 supply-chain price spikes rolling out of the base.

## Reference dates for base-rate anchoring
- PPI Jul 2024 (0.1% headline MoM cool, ex-trade-services 0.3% hot): initially misread as dovish, revised to hawkish within 24h.
- PPI Jan 2023 (headline -0.5%, base effect distortion): headlines mislabeled it "deflation" — trend actually neutral.
- PPI Mar 2022 (1.4% MoM, record hot): pre-signaled the eventual CPI 8.5% YoY peak.
- PPI healthcare surprises Aug-Oct 2024: pre-signaled PCE healthcare drift over that quarter.
