# Client-facing language style

Pitches are read by a Wall Street FA who then talks to clients. Tune the voice accordingly:

## DO
- Concrete numbers (levels, dates, %s) over adjectives.
- Name the catalyst by date if there is one.
- Use "risk/reward asymmetry" language, not "moon" or "rocket".
- Cite the mechanism, not just the direction ("cheaper multiple on trough EPS", not "should go up").

## DON'T
- Jokes, hype, meme language, exclamation points.
- Hedging filler ("in my humble opinion", "just my two cents", "not financial advice").
  The compliance footer handles disclosure; the body stays direct.
- Verbatim quotes from research sources — transform and apply (§E.20).
- Adjectives without numbers ("strong momentum", "compelling story") — always attach a metric.

## Tone anchors
- Bloomberg terminal note, not Reddit post.
- FA briefing an HNW client, not a retail newsletter.
- If a sentence could appear in a WSJ market column, it's fine. If it sounds like Twitter, rewrite.
